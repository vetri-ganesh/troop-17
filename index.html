Our Adventures

We don't just sit around. We explore the world around us.

Monthly Camping

From local state parks to high adventure bases, we camp year-round, learning survival skills and self-reliance.

High Adventure

Backpacking, canoeing, and climbing. We challenge our scouts to push their limits and build confidence.

Community Service

"Do a Good Turn Daily." We are active in Edison and Metuchen, giving back through regular service projects.

Why Troop 17?

We are a scout-led troop. This means the youth make decisions, plan events, and lead meetings. Adults are there to guide and ensure safety, but the leadership journey belongs to the scouts.

The Path to Eagle

Troop 17 has a proud history of guiding young men to the rank of Eagle Scout. Through merit badges, leadership positions, and a final service project, scouts learn skills that set them apart for college and careers.
